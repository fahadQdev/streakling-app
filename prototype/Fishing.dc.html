Daily trips · v0.9

Fishing, the Fish Book and the weekly league

Habits unlock the day’s trips. Health and Power win adventures, Stamina catches rarer fish, and the league lets people show off the effort.

The fishing screen

The hero stands on the dock with weapons put away, holding the equipped rod; every rod has its own colours. While a trip runs, the line is cast and the bobber bobs; clouds drift and the water sparkles all the time. In the last seconds a ! pops up for the bite. Shown here: the full Fish Book reward set.

Daily trips

Unlock: finish half of today’s due habits, rounded up (5 due → 3 done). A minimum day counts as done. A skipped habit stays in the total but does not count. Nothing due today: trips are open. No habits at all: locked.
Each day: 1 adventure and 1 fishing trip, one at a time. Unused trips do not carry over.
Bonus: every habit done before a trip starts: +25% adventure coins, or +1 fish.
Hero first: a new user creates the hero in onboarding, before the first habit.
Where: Today shows how many habits are left to unlock the trips; Character › Explore has Adventure, Fishing and the Fish Book.

Each trip

Length: 3 hours in the real app, 12 seconds in the demo, with a countdown on the painting.
Fish per trip: 1, then 2 from Lv 6, 3 from Lv 12, 4 from Lv 20. +1 when every habit was done first.
XP: +5 per fish, for the level and the weekly league.
Lures: paid when you cast, for one trip. A coin sink with a visible payoff.
Rods: Bamboo (free starter), Oak 150, Silver 400 (Lv 3), Coral 800 (Lv 5), Golden (Fish Book reward). All Stamina, in their own Rods tab.
Rarity: only Stamina and the lure change it. Health and Power do nothing here.

Weekly league

Bronze→Silver→Gold→Diamond
League: 20 players near your level, ranked by XP earned this week from check-ins and trips. It resets every week.
Moves: top 5 move up a tier, bottom 5 move down. Bronze is the floor, Diamond the top. New users start in Bronze.
Trophies: top 3 win a trophy; the first one unlocks the Champion Laurel (style only).
Friends: your friends ranked by weekly XP, with an invite button.
Hall of Fame: all-time top 3 worldwide for highest level, longest streak, Fish Book and rarest gear, with your own value under each list.
Player card: tap anyone to see their hero, level, weekly XP, best streak, badges, Fish Book and rarest gear, and to cheer them on.

Privacy and fair play

Never shown: habit names, amounts or notes. Only the hero and the totals above.
Hide me: one switch on your own card removes you from every board; you still see everyone else.
Cheers: a one-tap high five, once a week per player. No messages, nothing negative.
Caps: check-ins pay at most 60 coins and 60 XP a day, and trips only unlock through check-ins, so fake habits gain little.
Prototype: every other player is simulated. End the week (demo) shows promotion, trophies and the laurel.

Navigation

Bottom bar: Today · Stats · Character · Goals · Leaderboard. Character sits in the middle, raised, showing the hero’s face.
New habit: a + New habit button at the bottom right of Today only, in a slim bar with Reorder just above the tabs. The habit list stops above that bar, so the button never covers a habit’s check, + or timer button.
Settings: stay on the Today header.

Open questions

Real 3-hour trips: need a push notification when the hero is back, and a server clock so changing the phone time does not end a trip early.
League matchmaking: level is one input; time zone and activity matter too. Needs real data.
Rest days: trips stay open when nothing is due. Watch whether people schedule habits around that.
